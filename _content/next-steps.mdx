# Three Ways to Complete the Assignment

You've seen what happens when you generate lyrics from scores alone. Now you need to build a prompt chain that does better — one that preserves the poetic texture the spider chart compresses away.

Here are three ways to do that, from simplest to most involved. Pick the one that matches your comfort level and ambition.

---

## Option 1: Conversational Chat

**Best for:** Getting started quickly, or if you want to focus on the writing rather than the code.

You don't need to write any code to build a prompt chain. You can do the whole thing as a conversation in a chat interface — Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT all work.

The key is to **do it in stages**, saving each step's output before moving to the next:

1. **Paste each poem** and ask the model to score it on your traits (same as the web tool). Note the scores.
2. For each poem, ask: *"Extract the 3–5 most characteristic phrases from this poem. Describe its formal moves, emotional arc, and recurring sounds. Be specific and quote the text."*
3. Once you have 3+ poems analyzed, paste all the texture analyses together and ask: *"Synthesize a 150-word voice profile for this poet from these analyses."*
4. Finally, paste the voice profile + scores and ask: *"Write a love song in this poet's style."*
5. Refine: paste the draft back and add specific feedback — *"The chorus is too abstract. Revise with a concrete image from the poems."*

**For your essay:** Save your prompts at each step. You'll need to include them and explain why you made each choice.

1. Click the download link above — the file `complit126x-love-songs.ipynb` will save to your Downloads folder.
2. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com) and navigate to your **complit126x** folder.

4. Double-click the uploaded file — it will open in **Google Colab**.
5. In Colab, click the **🔑 Secrets** icon in the left sidebar. Your `OPENAI_API_KEY` should already be there from the Ancient Robots workshop — just make sure **Notebook access** is toggled ON. If you need to add it fresh, paste in your OpenAI key.
