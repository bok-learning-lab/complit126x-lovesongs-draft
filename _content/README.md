# Context Engineering

This section contains the readings and workshop guides for the **Love in Context** session of CompLit 126x.

## In this unit

- **[Learning Lab Intro to Context Engineering](/reading/learning-lab-intro-to-context-engineering)** — the assigned reading. What does an LLM actually see? Why does the spider chart produce flat lyrics? What is prompt chaining, and how does it help?

- **[Prompt Chaining: A Practical Guide](/reading/prompt-chaining-guide)** — workshop supplement. Step-by-step Python code for building a four-step chain that generates lyrics with actual poetic texture, not just scores.

## Before the workshop

1. Read the intro to context engineering.
2. Use the [Poem Personality Analyzer](/) to analyze at least three poems by your chosen poet.
3. Click **Generate First Draft Lyrics** and notice what the model produces from scores alone.
4. Bring your spider chart, your generated lyrics, and your questions to the session.
